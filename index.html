What's the difference between Agentic AI and Generative AI?

Generative AI creates content like text and images. Agentic AI takes action, executes workflows, and moves processes forward. While generative AI might write an email, Agentic AI could trigger that email based on prospect behavior, personalize it, update the CRM, and notify the sales team.

AI Search is growing but do I need it?

Yes, AI Search is growing fast! AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) helps structure your content so it clearly answers questions people ask in search engines. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) helps make your content easier for AI systems to understand, summarize, and reference. This helps your business appear in AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity.
