Agentic AI Versus Generative AI For Business Growth

Most people have heard of generative AI.

It is the technology behind tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and other platforms that generate content, answer questions, write copy, and create images.

Generative AI is powerful for content creation, brainstorming, research, and communication.

But there is a difference between generating content and taking action.

Agentic AI is designed to do more than generate.

It is designed to act, execute workflows, support decisions, and move processes forward.

While generative AI might write a follow-up email, Agentic AI could trigger that email based on a prospect action, personalize it based on behavior, schedule the follow-up task, update the CRM, and notify the sales team.

For business growth, the combination of generative AI and Agentic AI creates a more complete system.

Generative AI supports content, communication, and creativity.

Agentic AI supports execution, workflow, automation, and action.

My Global Mobile helps businesses use both types of AI in a way that supports real business outcomes.
